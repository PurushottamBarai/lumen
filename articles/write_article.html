1. The Fundamental Geometry of Partition Rebalancing

The historical implementation of consumer group management depended upon the "Eager Rebalance Protocol". Under this paradigm, every time a new consumer instances joined or left the pool, the entire consumer group surrendered its active partition leases before new mappings were determined.

verified_user
Key Architectural Invariant

Never allow consumer group rebalances to trigger stop-the-world partition assignment for mission-critical asynchronous workloads. By enabling the CooperativeStickyAssignor, individual partitions migrate iteratively without revoking unaffected consumer assignments.

terminal consumer.properties — Cooperative Sticky Configuration 
# Enforce Cooperative Sticky Partition Assignment
partition.assignment.strategy=org.apache.kafka.clients.consumer.CooperativeStickyAssignor
enable.auto.commit=false
max.poll.interval.ms=300000
session.timeout.ms=45000
heartbeat.interval.ms=10000
auto.offset.reset=earliest

2. Broker Durability Matrix Under Network Partitioning

The write durability of distributed logs is defined by the interaction between the producer acknowledge level and replication minimum thresholds. The matrix below defines behavior during broker quorum failures:

Acks Policy min.insync.replicas Broker Failures Tolerated (RF=3) Durability Guarantee
acks=0 1 None (Fire-and-forget) Zero Durability
acks=1 1 0 (Leader crash risks data loss) Leader-Only Ack
acks=all (-1) 2 1 broker outage surviving quorum Quorum Guaranteed
