Question 1

Which delivery guarantee does standard Kafka broker partitioning provide without supplementary clustering configuration?

check_circle Correct
Your Selection:
Option B: Strict FIFO ordering guaranteed strictly within a specific, single partition key stream.
psychology Author's Explanation:

Kafka guarantees strict FIFO message ordering exclusively within an isolated individual partition. Global ordering across distinct partitions requires external sequence coordination or dedicated single-partition topics.

Question 2

What does the min.insync.replicas=2 configuration enforce when acks=all?

check_circle Correct
Your Selection:
Option B: At least 2 replicas (including leader) must acknowledge the write before committing, preventing silent data loss upon leader crash.
psychology Author's Explanation:

When min.insync.replicas=2 and acks=all, writes fail with NotEnoughReplicasException if fewer than two ISR nodes are online, eliminating data loss during leader transition.

Question 3

Which rebalance protocol eliminates stop-the-world partition revocations in modern Kafka clients?

check_circle Correct
Your Selection:
Option A: Cooperative Sticky Assignment.
psychology Author's Explanation:

CooperativeStickyAssignor reallocates only reassigned partitions in successive rounds, leaving unaffected consumer partitions continuously processing.
