Which delivery guarantee does standard Kafka broker partitioning provide without supplementary clustering configuration?

Author's Technical Explanation

Kafka guarantees strict FIFO message ordering exclusively within an isolated individual partition. Global ordering across distinct partitions requires external sequence coordination or dedicated single-partition topics.
