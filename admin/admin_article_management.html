Deep Learning Architectures in Edge Systems

AR
Alex Rivera alex.rivera@techpulse.org
verified Verified Contributor
Submission Excerpt
This article examines lightweight inference runtimes and memory quantization techniques tailored for constrained edge devices. Focusing on INT8 and FP4 model compression, we benchmark latency, power consumption, and accuracy retention across embedded Linux clusters. Our comparative analysis details architectural trade-offs between tensor processing throughput and dynamic thermal dissipation in real-world deployments.
